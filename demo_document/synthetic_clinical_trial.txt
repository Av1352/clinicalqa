CLINICAL TRIAL SUMMARY REPORT

Trial Title: CARDIO-SHIELD Phase 2 — Efficacy and Safety of Velorapib in Patients with Stable Angina
Protocol Number: CS-2024-0117
Sponsor: Helix Therapeutics Inc.
Principal Investigator: Dr. Marcus Obi, MD, PhD
Site: Boston Medical Research Consortium
Trial Period: January 2024 – December 2025
Status: Completed

---

STUDY OVERVIEW

CARDIO-SHIELD evaluated the efficacy and safety of velorapib, a novel selective calcium channel modulator, compared to placebo in adult patients with stable angina pectoris. This was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.

---

ENROLLMENT

Total screened: 412 patients
Randomized: 280 patients
  - Velorapib 50mg arm: 140 patients
  - Placebo arm: 140 patients
Completed trial: 261 patients (93.2%)
Discontinued: 19 patients (6.8%) — 11 adverse events, 8 withdrawal of consent

Inclusion criteria: Age 40–75, documented stable angina, at least 2 episodes per week for 3 months prior.
Exclusion criteria: Recent MI within 6 months, LVEF below 40%, eGFR under 30 mL/min.

---

PRIMARY ENDPOINT

Reduction in weekly angina episode frequency at 24 weeks compared to baseline.

Velorapib 50mg: Mean reduction of 4.2 episodes per week (58% reduction from baseline)
Placebo: Mean reduction of 1.1 episodes per week (15% reduction from baseline)
Difference: 3.1 episodes/week (p < 0.001, 95% CI: 2.6–3.7)

Primary endpoint met. Statistically significant and clinically meaningful reduction in angina frequency.

---

SECONDARY ENDPOINTS

1. Time to first angina episode post-dose:
   Velorapib: 6.3 hours (median)
   Placebo: 2.1 hours (median)
   p = 0.003

2. Nitroglycerin rescue use per week:
   Velorapib: 0.8 uses/week
   Placebo: 2.9 uses/week
   p < 0.001

3. Quality of Life (Seattle Angina Questionnaire):
   Velorapib: +22.4 point improvement
   Placebo: +6.1 point improvement
   p = 0.008
